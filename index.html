Security & access

Who enters each piece of data, when, how it is verified, and who can see it.

RIDA does not move, hold, or approve restitution money. Payments continue through Georgia's existing court and DCS payment process. The partner bank reports what it paid; DCS confirms what posted. Mete holds no restitution funds and takes no percentage.

What each role can see and do

Safeguards

Sign-in
Named individual accounts with two-factor sign-in. No shared logins.
Access
Role-based; DCS approves DCS accounts, the partner bank approves its own staff.
Separation
DCS cannot edit partner records; the partner cannot edit DCS balances.
Audit
Every view, entry, decision and export is logged.
Consent
Signed participant consent covers DCS, RIDA and partner sharing.
Storage
U.S.-hosted, encrypted in transit and at rest. CJIS applicability to be confirmed.
Pilot end
Data returned or destroyed per the data-sharing agreement.
